## Remaining blocker

The layer-15 indexer score tensor is constructed by `build_qsa_sel` before `ggml_top_k`; no score snapshot or top-k margin was captured, so near-tie sensitivity and a direct causal chain are unproven. Actual logical GDN state needs sequence/cell/position-aware snapshots, including pending checkpoint and rollback row semantics; ordinary full sequence serialization would also copy the large KV cache. A safe bounded synchronized observer and controlled identical draft/target schedule across accepted and rejected transitions were not completed. Therefore there are no fixed-token pre-sampling logits, recurrent-state values, tolerances, or 98K correctness replay to report. Do not infer correctness from matching sampled output or acceptance history. GPU graph replay and mixed-sequence validation remain separate gates. Next work should capture layer-15 score margins and exact-input CPU reference, then implement and validate the bounded token/state observer before reconsidering model A/B.
