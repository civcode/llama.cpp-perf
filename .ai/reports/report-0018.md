## Unfinished boundary

The logical GDN R/S/P observer is **not implemented**. The recurrent `state_write` code resolves the sequence-owned logical cell via `rs_idx * size + (cell.src >= 0 ? cell.src : cell_index)`; after rollback the pending plane and cell ownership must be resolved at the exact post-`seq_rm` or checkpoint-restore boundary, and an async GPU read must follow synchronization. The new logits observer cannot stand in for this and does not provide GDN snapshots. A bounded per-layer/per-cell reader, manifest integration at pre-verification, post-accept and post-rollback/reprocessing checkpoints, and validation against a separately serialized recurrent-only reference remain necessary. Normal sampled histories happened to match here; if they diverge in future, the comparator fails and a separate explicitly diagnostic forced schedule is needed. No 98K model replay was attempted. Graph replay, mixed sequences and graph-safe overflow remain separate promotion gates.
