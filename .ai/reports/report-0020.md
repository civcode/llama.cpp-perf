## Decision

Report 0019 already observed significant sparse/dense recurrent and full-vocabulary drift. Exact identity is implausible as a general expectation with different dense-tile versus sparse-WMMA accumulation and rounding orders, although the evidence does not isolate a single arithmetic cause. Either establish a justified model-fidelity and task-quality criterion with appropriate controls, or modify the experimental sparse arithmetic to more closely match dense and remeasure. Neither direction authorizes an implicit tolerance or promotion. No full-model performance A/B, 98K replay, GitHub Actions, GPU reset or default promotion was performed.
